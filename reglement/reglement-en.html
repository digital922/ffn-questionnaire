SAINT-GERVAIS MONT-BLANC NATURE FILM FESTIVAL

OFFICIAL RULES 2027

1. Festival

The Saint-Gervais-Mont-Blanc Nature Film Festival is an event designed to foster a love of and understanding of nature, in a friendly, inquisitive and environmentally conscious spirit.

Through a competition featuring around twenty films, as well as access to talks, workshops and nature outings, the festival promotes a respectful approach to the natural world, combining artistic creation, ethical reflection and the sharing of knowledge.

The second edition will take place from 6 to 10 April 2027 in Saint-Gervais-les-Bains. It is organised in partnership with the Trophée Mer & Montagne.

The festival is aimed at a wide audience:

  • film and nature enthusiasts,
  • families and young audiences,
  • schoolchildren and students,
  • local residents and visiting tourists.

If we had to describe the festival in three key words:

  • Awe: to celebrate the beauty of life, spark curiosity and encourage the public to rethink their connection with nature through emotion and observation.
  • Sharing: the festival is a space for encounters and exchange, where the works invite everyone to question their own perspectives, compare viewpoints and contribute to a collective reflection on living things.
  • Protection: the festival highlights the challenges of preserving species and natural habitats, promotes positive initiatives and encourages respectful actions in support of biodiversity.

2. Films

Theme

The selected films:

  • focus primarily on nature and living organisms, from an international perspective,
  • invite viewers to reflect on the bonds that unite humans with nature, and on the need to protect biodiversity,
  • have a complete artistic freedom, whilst respecting animals and their environment (taking into account disturbance, imprinting, excessive baiting and threats to the species being filmed).

Genre

Films of all genres (documentary, fiction, essay, animation, educational, research, etc.) are eligible for selection.

Format

The selection includes short and feature-length films, with no maximum running time.

Production date

Films produced in 2023 or later are eligible.

Version

The festival is open to international films. All non-French-language films must be subtitled in French. French dubbing is not required.

3. Terms and Conditions

Entry

Each entrant may submit multiple entries. A separate entry must be made for each film.

Films currently in post-production are accepted, provided that the copy submitted gives a fair indication of the final quality of the work. If selected, the final version must be supplied by 31 January 2027. Failure to do so will result in the festival reserving the right to withdraw the film’s selection.

Commitment to Participation

Any entry to the festival constitutes a commitment by the entrant to maintain their film’s entry throughout the selection process and, if selected, until the festival takes place. The entrant undertakes not to withdraw their film after the call for entries has closed, unless there is a legitimate reason that is duly justified and subject to the organisation’s approval. In the event of an unjustified withdrawal following selection, the festival reserves the right to no longer accept submissions from the participant in future editions.

Call for entries

The call for entries is open from 19 October 2026 to 10 January 2027 at midnight. The festival reserves the right to extend the submission period.

Entry is free of charge and can be completed directly on the festival’s website.

The person submitting a film guarantees that they hold all the rights on behalf of the author(s) and director(s), or that they have the express authorisation of the production company or companies. The festival accepts no liability in the event of any dispute relating to the rights of the submitted works.

4. Film Selection

Selection process

The films admitted to the competition are selected by a selection committee, whose decisions are final and cannot be appealed.

The following are automatically excluded from selection:

  • films that are incomplete or deemed to be of insufficient artistic and/or technical quality,
  • films that do not comply with all the conditions set out in these regulations.

All entrants will be informed individually of the selection committee’s decisions.

The possibility of a member of the film crew attending the festival may be taken into account as one of the selection criteria.

Material to be submitted in the event of selection

In the event of selection, participants undertake to submit the following items to the festival, within the deadlines that will be communicated to them upon announcement of their selection:

  • A copy of the film in DCP format (if available);
  • The original digital master file in Full HD resolution (1920×1080), encoded in Apple ProRes 422 (or equivalent), at a frame rate of 25 frames per second and with a stereo mix (or 5.1 if available),
  • A trailer in Full HD format (1920×1080),
  • A minimum of three (3) stills from the film,
  • A minimum of one (1) photograph of the director(s).

The rights holders of the selected films grant the festival, free of charge, the right to use the stills and short extracts from the works for strictly promotional purposes. This licence shall cover the promotion of the 2027 edition of the festival, prior to, during and following the event, across all its communication channels, both print and digital (social media, website, press).

Retention and deletion of copies

Copies of films submitted to the festival for the purposes of selection and screening are used exclusively in connection with the organisation of the festival. Digital copies are deleted at the end of the event, with the exception of files that must be retained to comply with a legal obligation or in the event of a dispute. This deletion does not apply to promotional materials whose use is authorised by these regulations.

Films out of competition

The festival organisers may screen one or more films out of competition.

5. Film Competition

Awards

Five prizes, totalling at least 1,000 euros, are awarded to recognise the films presented and their artistic approaches. They are awarded by a jury of professionals following the screening of the films in competition. The list of prizes is as follows:

  • Festival Grand Prix,
  • Audience Award,
  • Short Film Award (under 26 minutes),
  • Conservation Award,
  • Trophée Mer & Montagne (please note that films competing in this category are subject to a separate call for entries).

The Festival reserves the right to change the number and titles of the prizes. Prizes cannot be held concurrently, with the exception of the Audience Award. All films are eligible for the Audience Award.

The jury may, if it so wishes, award one or more special mentions. The jury’s decisions are final.

Invitations

For selected films, the director or a member of the film crew will be invited to the Festival, subject to confirmation within the deadlines specified by the festival organisers.

Members of the film crew present at the festival undertake to take part in a Q&A session with the audience following the screening of their film.

Screening fees

The festival undertakes to pay a lump-sum screening fee of 100 euros in the event that no member of the selected film’s crew is able to attend. If at least one member of the crew attends, no screening fee will be payable.

6. General provisions and data protection

Acceptance of the rules

The submission of any work to the Saint-Gervais-Mont-Blanc Nature Film Festival implies full, complete and unreserved acceptance of these rules, as well as any organisational or operational adjustments required by the festival.

Personal Data Protection

Personal data collected during registration is used by the organisers of the Saint-Gervais-Mont-Blanc Nature Film Festival, managed by the Maison de Saint-Gervais, located at 43 rue du Mont-Blanc, 74170 Saint-Gervais-les-Bains, for the purposes of managing applications, selecting films, organising the event and following up on communications with participants. This data will not be transferred to or sold to third parties.

Data is retained for a maximum period of one year from the date of collection, after which it is deleted, unless there is a legal obligation requiring it to be retained for longer.

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable regulations, each participant has the right to access, rectify, erase and restrict the processing of their personal data, as well as, where applicable, the right to object. To exercise these rights or obtain further information on the processing of their data, participants may contact the organisation at festival@saintgervais.com or by telephone on 04 50 47 76 08.
